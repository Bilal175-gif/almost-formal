# Asset provenance

All product imagery in this development catalogue is generated demo content. The built-in image generation tool was used, not an API/CLI fallback. The images do not document actual inventory.

Saved assets:

- `apps/web/public/images/products.png`: original 4 × 2 photographic asset sheet.
- `apps/web/public/images/tie-0.webp` through `tie-7.webp`: individual catalogue images derived from the sheet.
- `apps/web/public/images/hero.png`: original generated hero.
- `apps/web/public/images/hero.webp`: optimized hero.
- `apps/web/public/fonts/`: locally hosted Google Fonts (Barlow Condensed, Caveat, DM Sans, Libre Caslon Display), downloaded from Google Fonts. These families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License; license files accompany the font assets.
- Stars, arrows, smiles and globe illustrations are original code-native SVGs in `components/doodles.tsx`.
- Paper grain is a code-native SVG turbulence texture in CSS.

## Product asset prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create a photographic asset sheet for Almost Formal vintage necktie store. Precisely 4 columns by 2 rows, eight equal rectangular product photographs in a clean edge-to-edge grid, NO gutters NO text NO labels NO border. Overall landscape 1536x1024. Each cell is an overhead photograph of ONE different vintage necktie laid folded lengthwise with its pointed wide blade facing down, entire tie fits inside its cell. Each photo background is beautiful warmly lit crumpled aged beige kraft paper with realistic creases, editorial vintage thrift aesthetic. Cell row 1 left to right: navy blue silk necktie with small gold and rust floral polka motifs; forest green silk necktie with thin diagonal mustard and rust red stripes; richly patterned patchwork paisley tie in navy red yellow; burgundy red tie with small gold blue geometric diamond motifs. Cell row 2 left to right: chocolate brown tie with subtle thin diagonal gold stripes; mustard yellow tie with blue botanical motifs; burgundy wine red vintage bow tie; navy blue tie with red and gold diagonal plaid stripes. Photorealistic finely textured silk, soft directional daylight and shadows, authentic slightly worn vintage pieces. No people or hands. Each cell independently composed. This is a photography sheet, not a website screenshot.

## Hero prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: large wide website hero background photograph, 1536x1024. Create an overhead flat lay of 5 beautiful real vintage silk neckties on warmly lit crumpled aged ivory parchment paper. Carefully arranged overlapping ties fill ONLY THE RIGHTMOST 57% of the photo, from top right to lower center, diagonal tilted ends and layers. The entire LEFT 43% must be plain empty softly textured aged cream paper, suitable for headline text to be overlaid in HTML. Ties are big and prominent: forest green with diagonal mustard and red stripes at front center, burgundy red paisley with gold and blue motifs to its right, navy blue with little gold floral motifs, red-blue vintage geometric print behind. Ties extend outside top edge, pointed ends at bottom right. Fine silk weave realistic fabric texture, authentic editorial vintage thrift photography. Warm beige paper texture with folds and wrinkles, earthy colors. Dark green torn paper barely visible along extreme left edge. No text, no typography, no illustration, no logos, no hands, no website interface. Natural side window light, tactile scrapbook mood, match a 1990s thrift ties flat lay.
